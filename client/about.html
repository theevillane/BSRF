Membership & rental rules (BSRF)

Dependents under 14 are registered on a guardian’s account. Youth 14–17 are full members. Adults 18–64 and senior citizens 65–70 use tiered fees. Members over 70 may hold membership but cannot rent bicycles; e-scooters follow the usual minimum age of 14. Bicycle rental requires the renter to be 70 or under; e-scooter rental requires 14 or over.
